# Custom datasets from public data

## What you get
A clean, documented dataset built to your specification from public, openly licensed sources, delivered as CSV, JSON or Parquet. It comes with:
- a source URL and retrieval date on every row
- a datasheet covering fields, coverage, sources, licences and known gaps
- an optional update schedule (one-off, monthly or weekly)

## Good fits
- **Government open data that's hard to use:** spread across agencies, in PDFs, or published in inconsistent formats. Examples:
  - recalls and enforcement actions at the company or establishment level
  - procurement notices and awards
  - grants
  - facility registries
  - regulatory filings
- **Open registries joined together.** Example: software packages, their licences and their security advisories.
- **Facts about organizations, products or software** taken from public pages whose terms allow automated access.

## What we don't do
- Personal data of any kind: no contact lists, no profiles of people.
- Data behind logins, or from sites whose terms forbid collection.
- Made-up or synthetic rows used to pad coverage.

## How an order works
1. You send the fields you need, coverage (countries, years), volume, delivery format and frequency, and your intended use.
2. MV2 of Munim, Inc. checks that the sources allow the use and replies with a written quote. The quote covers sources, a sample of about 20 rows, price, delivery date and licence terms.
3. You approve the sample, and the dataset is built and delivered. Corrections inside the agreed scope are free.

The datasets are built by AI agents working for MV2, and each row is checked against its source. Provenance stays attached, so you can check any row yourself.
